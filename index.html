Capital Creation Studio · v0.6
Commercial opportunity and capital-generation laboratory
ILLUSTRATIVE · LOCAL ONLY

      
Compare three candidate business initiatives against the same transparent cash-flow rules. Estimate cash distributable to your ownership stake, initial capital at risk, hypothetical payback and downside funding needs. This is not an investment recommendation or business valuation.

      
Model integrity: Cash distributions are shown separately from the value of an ownership stake. Working-capital reserves are retained in the business, not treated as spendable income. Do not simultaneously count distributions as growing business equity and as invested cash in Capital Intelligence v0.5.

      

        
        
        
      

      






      

        Advanced assumptions: growth, tax, financing, ownership and working capital
        









      

      

      

      
Three-way opportunity comparison

      
Downside assumes 65% of projected sales; upside assumes 130%. Fixed operating costs, margins and capital requirements are unchanged. Payback is withheld if modeled funding deficits occur.
